## 5. Localization Considerations (non-SERP)

### Terminology
- **Drop all romaji.** *mōshiwake gozaimasen* → 申し訳ございません(でした), *hansei* → 反省, *iiwake* → 言い訳, *hou-ren-sou* → 報連相, *uchi-soto* → 社内・社外. These are self-evident to JA readers; the base article's gloss-and-explain treatment becomes filler.
- JA-native heavier tiers the base barely uses: 謹んでお詫び申し上げます / 不徳の致すところ / 監督不行き届き — expected in serious/role-responsibility cases.

### Examples to rewrite
- Replace the base's "engineer reports a bug" / Slack-first framing with the JA SERP's stock scenes: 連絡忘れ, 納期遅延, 請求・数値ミス, 添付の誤送信, クレーム報告 — delivered as **full email templates** (社内 + 社外), not spoken one-liners.
- The "same incident across 4 channels" device doesn't fit JA manner norms; convert to a channel-*hierarchy* note.

### Register / tone
- **Reversed problem.** The base corrects *over-formalization* by a non-native. A JA-native article must correct the *opposite* (too casual: すみません-only, 了解です, スタンプ) — the felissimo angle. The base's centerpiece calibration table is not just unhelpful in JA, it's backwards.

### Cannibalization (the decisive factor)
- **`how-to-say-sorry-in-japanese-politely` is already JA-published.** A JA 上司謝罪 article targets the *same* JA-native audience and the *same* speech act → near-duplicate, internal cannibalization.
- Also overlaps JA keigo cluster (`keigo-guide` / `keigo-mistakes` / `keigo-cheat-sheet` / `polite-japanese-phrases-for-office`) and `common-japanese-business-mistakes.ja`.
- The JA "上司 謝罪 メール" SERP is a crowded, high-DA market (Mynavi, All About, 三越伊勢丹, cybozu, テンプレ集) where our **language-learning-for-foreigners moat does not apply**. We would compete on generic business-manner depth against incumbents — off-strategy.

### Local expert references (only if JA is pursued)
- Pair with native-manager sourcing; cite JA business-manner conventions (件名規則, 締めの定型) rather than learner-facing explanations.

### Strategic fork (for human decision)
1. **Defer JA (recommended).** EN-only for this slug now; the JA-native apology need is already served by the JA-published sibling + keigo cluster. Revisit only if GSC shows real JA demand our existing JA articles miss.
2. **Merge, don't add.** If anything, fold a short "上司に謝るとき" angle into the *existing* JA `how-to-say-sorry-in-japanese-politely` rather than ship a competing JA URL.
3. **Full Option-B pivot.** Write a JA-native 上司謝罪メール article — but this means competing head-on in a saturated high-DA niche off our moat, and accepting cannibalization with our own JA sibling. Not recommended.

This mirrors the JA decisions on `japanese-business-phrases` / `japanese-honorifics-chart` / `common-japanese-business-mistakes` (sharp divergence + native-audience pivot → defer or Option B-with-caution). See `[[feedback_localize_business_growth_frame]]`.
