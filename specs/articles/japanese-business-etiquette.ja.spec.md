## 5. Localization Considerations (non-SERP)

### Terminology
- Romaji is dropped for the JA reader; terms appear in their native kanji/kana: 敬語 / 尊敬語・謙譲語・丁寧語 / 名刺 / 根回し / 稟議 / 報連相 / 上座・下座 / 会釈・敬礼・最敬礼 / 飲み会 / お中元・お歳暮.
- **ウチ／ソト** in katakana (house rule established in `sonkeigo-vs-kenjougo.ja.spec.md`), not romaji *uchi-soto*.
- The base spec's "A/B/C" register stays as a compact in-text label only (per house rule: A→B→C on first mention, then 「段階A/B/C」), and **must not appear in the title** (`feedback_title_no_internal_jargon`).
- "Tier 1/2/3" → JA: 「重要度（取引を壊す／印象に響く／些細）」. Per `feedback_no_fixed_yen_in_rubrics`, keep severity qualitative (業務・信頼への影響), no yen.

### Examples to rewrite
- The base "Say it (C):" phrase callouts become natural JA lines without romaji — e.g. 名刺受領時「頂戴いたします」、上司を客に言うとき「弊社の田中がご説明いたします」、会議冒頭「本日はお忙しい中、お時間をいただきありがとうございます」、カメラオフ「カメラを切らせていただきます」.
- The foreigner-calibration table reframes to JA reader voice: 「外国人なら大目に見てもらえること（お辞儀の角度・発音・地域差）」 vs.「絶対に外せないこと（時間厳守・名刺の扱い・客前で自社上司に -san を付けない・間接的なNoを誤読しない）」. Do **not** invert it to an HR-manager "what to forgive in foreigners" table — the reader is the foreigner.

### Register / tone
- Reader = foreign professional reading Japanese (likely JLPT N3↑). Use plain, clear 敬体 (です・ます), gloss in-line where a term is advanced, avoid talking down. This matches f-ship / vnservice tone (平易な敬語) rather than the HR-whitepaper tone of meikoglobal.
- Pillar primer framing: 「把握」「全体像」「地図」 — NOT 「復習」 (`feedback_pillar_first_touch_framing`: this is a cluster pillar = first-touch).

### Local expert references
- Optional authority: 文化庁『敬語の指針』(2007) for the 敬語 line (precedent: `sonkeigo-vs-kenjougo.ja`). For 報連相 / 名刺 / 席次, no single canonical source — keep prescriptive but grounded.
